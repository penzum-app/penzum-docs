Penzum is a financial and bookkeeping app built for Hungarian sole traders (egyéni vállalkozók). You don't necessarily need a separate accountant to always have a clear view of your income, expenses, and tax burden — Penzum does the calculations, keeps the statutory records, and tracks your deadlines, all in one place.

## Who is it for?

Penzum is designed for **flat-rate tax (átalányadó), KATA, and itemised entrepreneurial income tax (VSZJA) sole traders** — whether full-time, part-time, or alongside a pension.

## Where Penzum's scope ends

Penzum handles record-keeping and calculations, but you still need to submit your filings yourself (NAV ENYA, eSZJA) — Penzum doesn't submit them on your behalf. If you do keep an accountant, you can back up your figures precisely with reports exported from Penzum.
