---
title: Deleting your data
description: Permanently deleting your account and data
---

If you want to permanently delete your account and all stored data, you can start this under **Settings → Account → Delete Account**.

## What gets deleted?

- All recorded invoices and income
- All expense records and bank transactions
- All settings and personal data
- AI-generated tips and history

## Important notes

The deletion process is **irreversible**: once confirmed, your data cannot be restored. We must keep some data after deletion: your e-mail address for 30 days after the deletion, and the log of your acceptance of the Terms and the payment identifiers for 5 years. Details: the privacy notice.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent. You must keep your receipts until the right to assess the tax becomes time-barred: please download your data before proceeding.
</Warning>

## Downloading your data before deletion

The **Settings → Account → Download my data (JSON)** button lets you download all your stored data, at any time, in a machine-readable JSON format, under Article 20 of the GDPR — including personal data, invoices, expenses, employment entries, voluntary pension contributions, the life-event log, and tax obligations.

## GDPR

Penzum handles your data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The privacy policy is available at [penzum.app/privacy](https://penzum.app/privacy).
