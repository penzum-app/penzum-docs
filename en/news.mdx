The Penzum News widget shows daily-refreshed, relevant professional news on the Dashboard — no need to search for it yourself.

## What sources?

The content comes from Hungarian tax and invoicing trade portals, as well as official NAV announcements.

## Categories

Every news item is tagged with a category:

| Category | Covers |
|---|---|
| Taxation | General tax news |
| NAV | Official announcements, guidance |
| Invoicing | Invoicing rules, eÁFA |
| Finance | General financial news |

## Relevance

News is tailored to your tax form — KATA and flat-rate taxpayers see different content prioritized.
