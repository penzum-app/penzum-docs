The **How am I doing?** page (`/hogy-allok`) offers a more compact, one-page yearly summary than the Dashboard — answering the question of how your business is doing this year. It's split into three sections.

## This year's picture

Year-to-date collected revenue and monthly average, plus a **comparison to the same period last year** (↑/↓ badge, calculated only over the elapsed days of both years for fairness). Under VSZJA, you'll see a warning if your expenses significantly exceed your revenue.

## Tax situation

Just the facts: your year-to-date estimated tax burden, plus a green/red indicator showing whether any payment-type obligation recorded in Penzum is overdue and not yet marked as paid. If there are arrears, a link takes you to [Obligations](/en/obligations) for details.

## Trends

If your current pace continues, what your projected annual revenue would be — and if that would cross your tax form's revenue ceiling (for flat-rate tax, ten times the annual minimum wage; for KATA, the HUF 18 million threshold), an estimated crossing date. For flat-rate and KATA sole traders, it also shows AAM (VAT-exemption) utilization as a progress bar.
