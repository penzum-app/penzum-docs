---
title: KATA
description: The itemised tax for small businesses in Penzum
---

KATA (kisadózók tételes adója — the itemised tax for small businesses) is a simplified tax scheme where you pay a fixed monthly amount regardless of your actual revenue, up to an annual ceiling.

## Monthly KATA payment

The monthly tax is fixed and does not depend on revenue:

- **Full-time sole trader:** 50,000 HUF/month
- **Part-time sole trader:** 25,000 HUF/month

For a full year, this amounts to **600,000 HUF** in total tax — even if your revenue approaches the annual ceiling.

## Annual revenue ceiling

The statutory annual revenue ceiling is **18,000,000 HUF**. Exceeding this limit triggers additional tax obligations on the surplus amount.

Penzum tracks your year-to-date revenue against this ceiling on the Dashboard, showing how much headroom you have remaining.

## Limit alerts

Penzum automatically notifies you when your revenue reaches **80%** or **100%** of the annual ceiling — via email and push notification.

<Info>
  Limit alerts are available on the **Navigator** plan.
</Info>

## Obligations calendar

Your monthly KATA payment deadlines appear automatically on the Obligations page. You can mark each payment as completed using the standard three-step checkbox system.

## Switching tax forms

Switching between KATA and átalányadó (flat-rate tax) is only possible with effect from **1 January**. A mid-year exit from KATA would trigger vállalkozói SZJA (entrepreneurial personal income tax) for the transitional period, which Penzum does not currently handle.

You can record a tax form change on the **Life Events** page — Penzum will automatically set the date to 1 January and remind you of the required filing steps.

## Special restrictions

KATA has specific legal restrictions, for example on transactions with affiliated parties. Penzum does not validate these rules — consult your accountant for guidance on related-party situations.
