title: KATA
description: The itemised tax for small businesses in Penzum
---

KATA (kisadózók tételes adója — the itemised tax for small businesses) is a simplified tax scheme where you pay a fixed monthly amount regardless of your actual revenue, up to an annual ceiling.

## Monthly KATA payment

The monthly tax is fixed and does not depend on revenue: **50,000 HUF/month** as a full-time sole trader — for a full year, this amounts to **600,000 HUF** in total tax, even if your revenue approaches the annual ceiling.

<Info>
  KATA can currently only be selected for **full-time** sole traders in Penzum.
</Info>

## Annual revenue ceiling

The statutory annual revenue ceiling is **18,000,000 HUF**. If you exceed it, a 40% surtax is payable on the revenue above the ceiling (Katv. 7. § (4), (5)); your KATA status does not end because of this.

Penzum tracks your year-to-date revenue against this ceiling on the Dashboard, showing how much headroom you have remaining.

## Limit alerts

Penzum automatically notifies you when your revenue reaches **80%** or **100%** of the annual ceiling — via email and push notification.

## Obligations calendar

Your monthly KATA payment deadlines appear automatically on the Obligations page. You can mark each payment as completed using the standard three-step checkbox system.

## Switching tax forms

KATA status ends on the last day of the month in which you report the exit (Katv. 5. § (1) a)); you can move to KATA from the first day of the month following your report (Katv. 4. § (1)). You cannot choose KATA again in the year in which it ended or in the following 12 months (Katv. 6. §). Flat-rate tax (átalányadó) is chosen for the whole tax year (Szja tv. 50. § (1)); a sole trader leaving KATA can report the application of flat-rate taxation together with the notification of the KATA exit, on form T101E, provided they meet all the conditions of the Szja tv. at the time of the notification (Katv. § 12 (5)). Penzum does not advise on switching tax forms and does not file the notification for you: ask NAV or your accountant about the exact steps.

You can record a tax form change on the [Life Events](/en/life-events) page.

## Special restrictions

KATA comes with specific conditions: KATA status ends, among other things, if you receive income from goods or services from a payer (kifizető) within the meaning of the Art. (the Hungarian Act on the Rules of Taxation), including a foreign payer, or if you are no longer a main-occupation sole trader (Katv. 5. § (1) g), h)). Penzum checks the main-occupation status when you select KATA, but afterwards it does not continuously monitor changes in that status or income from payers: if in doubt, ask NAV or your accountant.
