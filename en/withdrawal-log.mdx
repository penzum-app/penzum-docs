<Info>
  The Withdrawal Log applies to the **itemised entrepreneurial income tax (VSZJA)** tax form.
</Info>

An entrepreneur withdrawal is the amount you pay out to yourself, as a sole trader, from your business revenue. It reduces your entrepreneurial tax base, but is subject to personal income tax (15%), social security contribution (18.5%), and social contribution tax (13%).

## Where to find it

Under **Records → Withdrawal Log**, at the `/nyilvantartasok/kivet` page.

## Monthly entry

The page shows 12 rows, one per month. Future months aren't editable, and the current month is highlighted.

For a given month, click **Record** (or **Edit** if an entry already exists) and enter:

- **Amount (HUF)**
- **Note** *(optional)*

After saving, the amount appears on that month's row. If you didn't withdraw anything in a given month, leave it blank — or, if you recorded an amount by mistake, remove it with the **Delete** button.

<Info>
  Only **one** entry can be recorded per month. If you withdrew money in several installments during a month, enter the combined total.
</Info>

## Summary

Two cards at the top of the page show:
- **Total annual withdrawal** — the sum of everything recorded this year
- **Recorded months** — how many months already have an entry (N/12)

## Related pages

Alongside withdrawals, VSZJA also requires keeping a [Fixed Assets](/en/fixed-assets) register, and — if you have any cash transactions — a [Cash Book](/en/cash-book). The annual total also appears in the Income Register report under [Reports](/en/reports).
