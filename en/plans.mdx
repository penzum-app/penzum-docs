Penzum is available as a **single, all-inclusive subscription** — there's no limited-feature base tier, and no guessing which plan is right for you. Every feature is available from day one.

## Price

**HUF 9,990/month**, or **HUF 99,900/year** with annual billing (2 months free compared to monthly).

## Trial

<Info>
  After signing up, choose between a **30-day free trial** with a card on file (nothing is charged until the trial ends), or immediate **annual billing** with a 2-month discount. Both can be cancelled anytime.
</Info>

## What's included?

- Dashboard — income, expenses, tax, and free balance in one place
- Online invoicing sync (Billingo, Számlázz.hu, and other systems)
- NAV Online Invoice sync
- Categorized expense tracking, with receipt photos
- Tax obligation calendar, with email and push reminders (7 and 1 days before deadline)
- AAM (VAT-exemption threshold) monitor with early warnings
- Actual + scenario tax calculator
- AI Advisor (including document attachments)
- Step-by-step form guidance per obligation and life event
- CSV bank statement import (automatic bank sync coming soon)
- PDF export — tax summaries and reports

## Why only one plan?

Penzum is a single, all-inclusive subscription — there are no limited-feature tiers. Every feature is available from day one of your subscription, regardless of whether you're a part-time, full-time, or retired sole trader.

<Info>
  Manage your subscription — billing details, cancellation — under **Settings → Subscription**. See [Managing your subscription](/en/subscription).
</Info>
