The Obligations page gives you an overview of upcoming and overdue tax payment tasks.

## How does an obligation appear?

Penzum automatically generates expected obligations based on your tax form, business type, and income — following statutory deadlines.

The chamber contribution (HUF 5,000 a year, due by March 31 of the tax year, payable to the national chamber of commerce and industry) appears among your obligations for tax forms other than KATA; for KATA, Penzum does not show this row, but you still have to pay the contribution. If you suspend your business activity for the entire calendar year and notify the chamber of this, the law does not require you to pay the contribution (Gkt. section 34/A (1)). Penzum shows the row regardless of any suspension and does not take the exemption into account: check with the chamber whether it applies to you.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Upcoming | There's still time to complete it |
| Due | The deadline is within 30 days |
| Overdue | The deadline has passed, not marked paid |
| Paid | Marked as completed |

## Marking as paid

Once you've paid a tax, mark it as paid — it then disappears from the active list.

## Email notifications

Penzum sends email reminders about upcoming deadlines to your registered email address.

<Warning>
  The deadlines shown by Penzum are for informational purposes. Always verify the actual filing and payment deadlines with NAV or your accountant.
</Warning>
