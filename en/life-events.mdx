---
title: Life Events
description: Logging changes to your tax status
---

The Life Events page (`/life-events`) lets you record changes that affect your tax status — Penzum then automatically applies the correct calculation logic across the Dashboard, the Calculator, and the Obligations calendar.

## Recordable event types

The page only allows events from a **closed list**:

- **Business type change** (part-time ↔ full-time ↔ retired)
- **Tax form change** (flat-rate ↔ KATA ↔ VSZJA)
- **Child born** / **Dependent ended**
- Recording a **marriage**
- **Disability** start / end
- **GYED** (childcare fee) start / end
- **GYES** (childcare allowance) start / end
- **Sick leave** start / end
- **Business suspension** start / end

<Info>
  The page only shows buttons relevant to your current status — e.g. "Record marriage" only appears if you're not already recorded as married.
</Info>

## Business type change

Effective date, new business type (excluding your current one), and an optional note. An amber warning reminds you that you also need to notify NAV separately — Penzum does not do this on your behalf.

## Tax form change

Effective date and the new tax form. The rules differ by tax form: flat-rate tax and entrepreneurial income taxation (VSZJA) are chosen for the whole tax year (Szja tv. 50. § (1)); KATA status ends on the last day of the month in which you report the exit, and you can move to KATA from the first day of the month following your report (Katv. 5. § (1) a), 4. § (1)). Penzum does not advise on switching tax forms and does not file the notification for you.

## Child born / Dependent ended

Date, an optional checkbox if the child has a disability, and a note. This entry updates your dependent count, which is the basis for the family tax benefit calculation.

## Marriage, disability, GYED, GYES, sick leave, business suspension

These all have the same shape: date and an optional note. Marriage can only be recorded once. You can record the period of maternity pay (CSED) and adoption pay with the GYED event as well: for the TB and szocho lower limit, the law treats them the same way.

## Log

At the bottom of the page, every past event is listed chronologically, with its state transition (e.g. "Part-time → Full-time"), date, and — where relevant — a note about whether an official filing is also required (e.g. with NAV).

<Warning>
  Life Events does **not** replace the [Employment](/en/employment) page — if you have a job (or its end) at another company, it cannot be recorded here, only on the Employment page.
</Warning>
