Penzum Tip shows a fresh, personalized financial tip on the Dashboard every day — built from your income, expenses, and obligations.

## What's in a tip?

Tips cover a range of topics:
- Highlighting upcoming tax payment deadlines
- Keeping the annual revenue ceiling (VAT-exemption threshold) top of mind
- General sole-trader financial habits and advice
- Current legislative changes for the year

## How does it update?

Tips refresh automatically once a day. Use the "Next tip" button to see the rest of the day's tips.

## Important

Penzum Tip is for informational purposes only and does not constitute tax advice.
