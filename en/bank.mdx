---
title: Bank Account Connection
description: Automatic transaction import from your bank account
---

Bank account connection is a subscription feature (available during the trial period as well).

## Connection steps

1. On the **Bank** page (or the **Settings → Bank Account** card), click the **Csatlakoztatás** (Connect) button. Penzum gives you a code.
2. On the **bankszamlakivonat.hu** partner site, connect your bank account (if you have not done so yet), then enter the code you received from Penzum in the **Számlatörténet megosztása** (Share account history) menu, and choose what you share with Penzum.
3. Return to Penzum and click the **Frissítés** (Refresh) button on the screen that shows the code, so that Penzum sees that you entered the code.

## What does Penzum receive?

Penzum receives the transactions and the balance of your bank account through the service of Nyíltbankolás Zrt. (bankszamlakivonat.hu). You register and give the consent to your bank on the provider's own interface, and you allow the sharing with Penzum by entering the code you received from Penzum.

Penzum receives account information only (transactions and balance) and does not initiate payments. Penzum does not receive or store your online banking login details (username, password, PIN).

## Refresh

Transactions are refreshed daily; the provider does not guarantee the most recent data.

## Supported banks

The list of supported banks is shown on the bankszamlakivonat.hu interface; the list may change. If your bank is not on it, you can also upload your bank statement as a CSV file.

## Transactions, income and expenses

Imported transactions do not automatically become income or expenses: you can match an item to an invoice or to an expense, or create an expense from it. Penzum does its tax calculation from your invoices and expenses.

## Alternative: CSV import

You can also import your bank statement in CSV format. Penzum currently recognizes the CSV formats of Revolut, OTP, Wise and CIB.
