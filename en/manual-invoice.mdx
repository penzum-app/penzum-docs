title: Manual invoice entry
description: Adding an invoice or receipt by hand, for your own records
---

If you don't use connected invoicing software, you can also record your income manually — either as an Invoice or a Receipt type.

<Warning>
  An Invoice or Receipt recorded manually in Penzum is **your own record**, not an official
  issuance. A legally valid invoice must exist in an actual invoicing system (e.g. Billingo,
  Számlázz.hu); a legally valid receipt must be issued through a cash register/e-cash
  register or a paper receipt pad. Penzum does not replace issuance today — it only helps
  you keep track.
</Warning>

## Adding a new invoice

1. Go to the **Income** page
2. Click **New Invoice**
3. Fill in the fields:
   - Client name
   - Net amount (HUF)
   - Issue date
   - Payment due date
4. After saving, the invoice enters **Sent** status

## Marking as paid

Once your client's payment arrives, select the invoice and click **Paid** — its status becomes **Paid**. Only then does it count toward your income summary and tax estimate.

## Alongside invoicing software

Manual and synced invoices can coexist — Penzum handles both.

## Planned improvement

A future feature (not yet built) will let Penzum trigger real, official invoice issuance
directly through your connected invoicing software — until then, manual entry is for your
own records only.
