title: Manual invoice entry
description: Adding an invoice by hand, without invoicing software
---

If you don't use connected invoicing software, you can also record your income manually.

## Adding a new invoice

1. Go to the **Income** page
2. Click **New Invoice**
3. Fill in the fields:
   - Client name
   - Net amount (HUF)
   - Issue date
   - Payment due date
4. After saving, the invoice enters **Pending** status

## Marking as paid

Once your client's payment arrives, select the invoice and click **Paid**. Only then does it count toward your income summary and tax estimate.

## Alongside invoicing software

Manual and synced invoices can coexist — Penzum handles both.
