The Dashboard is Penzum's home page — all your important data in one place.

## Left side — main content

### Income and expense summary
A summary of the current calendar year's income and expenses, broken down by month.

### Obligations
Tax payment and filing deadlines due in the next 60 days. Overdue obligations are shown in red, upcoming ones in yellow.

### Invoices and transactions
A quick overview of your latest invoices and bank transactions.

## Right side — sidebar

### Connections
The status of your integrations, in one place:

| Row | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Számlázz.hu / Billingo | Last sync time, if active |
| NAV Online Invoice | Last sync time, if active |
| Bank sync | Coming soon |

Green checkmark: active sync. Grey circle: not connected or inactive.

### Penzum News
Automatically collected news relevant to sole traders — on taxation, invoicing, and the latest NAV announcements.

### Penzum Tip
A daily, personalized financial tip — built from your own data. Use the "Next tip" button to page through the day's tips.
