After signing in for the first time, a short onboarding questionnaire collects your basic details — needed for tax estimates and personalization.

## Step 1 — Basic details

Last name, first name, tax ID, TEÁOR activity code *(optional)*, and your business start date.

## Step 2 — Tax form and business type

Choose the [tax form](/en/tax-form) that applies to your business (flat-rate tax, KATA, or VSZJA — KATA can only be selected for full-time sole traders) and your business type:

| Type | When to choose |
|---|---|
| Full-time | Your business is your sole source of income |
| Part-time | You also have an active employment contract |
| Retired | You run your business alongside a pension |

If you're unsure about the tax form, ask your accountant.

## Step 3 — Personal details

Gender, date of birth, disability status, and — if relevant — your first marriage date. These are needed for accurate tax-benefit calculations.

## Step 4 — Children

Number of dependent children, and how many of them live with a disability, if any. (The number of **beneficiary** children — if it differs from your total dependents — can be fine-tuned later under **Settings → Personal Details**.)

## Step 5 — Summary

A read-only recap of everything you entered, then save — this moment becomes the starting entry in your [Life Events](/en/life-events) log.
