---
title: Flat-rate tax
description: The basics of flat-rate tax (átalányadó) for sole traders
---

Under flat-rate tax, a statutory cost-ratio percentage is deducted from your revenue — the remainder is your tax base, on which tax is due.

## Annual revenue ceiling

Flat-rate taxation has an annual revenue ceiling: ten times the annual minimum wage (fifty times if, for the entire tax year, you carry out retail activity exclusively). If you start, close or suspend your business during the year, the ceiling is pro rata; Penzum does not calculate this yet and measures the bar against the full annual ceiling. If you exceed it, you lose your eligibility for flat-rate taxation and must switch to taxation based on business income (vállalkozói jövedelem) for the entire tax year. You cannot choose flat-rate taxation again for the year in which it ended or for the following 12 months.

On the web Dashboard, the flat-rate tax bar changes color at 70% and 90% of the full annual ceiling; on the Dashboard and on the [How am I doing?](/en/how-am-i-doing) page, Penzum tells you if, at your current pace, you would exceed the ceiling by the end of the year. The pro rata ceiling is not taken into account. In the mobile app, the How am I doing? screen shows your projected annual revenue and also around when, at your current pace, you would reach the limit (with the name and the amount of the limit).

## Tax types

As a flat-rate taxpayer, you need to account for:

- **SZJA** — personal income tax
- **TB contribution** — for full-time sole traders
- **Social contribution tax** — for full-time sole traders
- **HIPA** — local business tax *(optional, if enabled)*

Tax burdens differ for part-time sole traders — Penzum calculates based on your business type.

## Tax benefits

Certain life situations allow you to claim a tax benefit (e.g. for children, long-term illness, or first marriage). These can be set under **Settings → Personal Details**.
